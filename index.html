About

Chienn Tai (@desigh.llc) is a Taiwanese composer, pianist, and multimedia artist based in New York. Classically trained in piano and dance, her work moves between film scoring, orchestral ambient production, and live scoring with electroacoustic elements. Her work has been presented at Ars Electronica Festival (Austria), Paseo Project Festival, and SVA Theatre premiere, with performances at Carnegie Hall and National Concert Hall (Taiwan). She holds an MPS from NYU Tisch School of the Arts and serves on the board of directors at the Center on Contemporary Art, Seattle.

Linus Chen is a music producer and sound engineer. Having his roots in the electronic music scene as a DJ and producer, Linus brings a different approach to the contemporary projects he works on. Linus takes inspiration from house music production techniques and sound design, giving the productions he works on a different sonic character.
